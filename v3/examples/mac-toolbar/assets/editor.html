A good day has room around it. Leave the phone at home. Walk until the city sounds different. Buy something warm on the way back. # The long way home Take the street with the old trees, even though it adds twenty minutes. Notice the windows left open above the bakery and the bicycles leaning against every second fence. At the market, choose the peaches by scent instead of colour. Carry them carefully. Let the paper bag warm in your hands. There is no prize for arriving early today. Sit near the water until the light changes, and allow the afternoon to become evening without asking it to be useful. When you finally turn home, leave enough quiet in the day to hear your own footsteps.
